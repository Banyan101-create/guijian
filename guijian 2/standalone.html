The sun follows its real path over Beijing at the equinox: up due east at 06:00, fifty degrees high at noon, down due west at 18:00. After dusk the full moon rises in the east, the stars come out, and the palace lights its lanterns and windows. Raking light at 卯时 and 酉时 reads the 举架 curve and the corner sweep best; 午时 flattens them. 精细 adds contact shadow to every still frame once the camera stops moving.
